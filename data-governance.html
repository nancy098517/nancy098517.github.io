一步錯，步步錯：為什麼資料治理是企業風控的命脈？

企業投入大量資源導入 ERP、大數據分析與人工智慧，期望提升效率與決策品質。然而，在導入新系統之前，有一個更基本的問題值得先問：

我們能信任系統裡的資料嗎？

「Garbage in, garbage out」（垃圾進，垃圾出）提醒我們：當源頭資料失真，後續分析再精密，也可能把錯誤包裝成看似可靠的決策依據。

從身分與權限管理，看見資料可信度

以最基本的身分與權限管理為例，企業值得持續檢視：

  • 離職人員的系統權限是否及時撤銷？
  • 留停或調職人員的權限是否依實際需要調整？
  • 帳號、卡片或其他身分憑證，是否由本人使用？
  • 系統留下的操作紀錄，能否追溯到實際執行者？

當憑證被轉交、帳號被共用，系統記錄的「誰做了什麼」，就可能與事實產生落差。這會影響審計軌跡的可信度；相關紀錄可能需要進一步驗證，才能作為責任釐清、風險分析與管理決策的依據。

資料治理不只是技術問題

要改善這些問題，需要技術控制，也需要管理上的承諾。當異常被發現時，組織願不願意確認原因、修正流程，並持續追蹤？還是只希望報表準時交出、KPI 維持漂亮？

資料治理要落實，需要幾個基本動作：

  1. 建立到職、調職、留停與離職的權限管理流程。
  2. 避免共用帳號與轉交身分憑證，保留可追溯的操作紀錄。
  3. 定期覆核權限，追蹤異常與改善結果。
  4. 讓提出問題的人，能安心回報並參與改善。

AI 時代，更需要可信任的資料

當 AI 開始參與企業的預測、分析與決策，資料品質、權限管理與可追溯性，更應成為導入前的基本功。這不只是資訊部門的工作，也需要人力資源、財務、稽核及管理階層共同參與。

如果組織排除的是「提出問題的人」，資料問題可能只是從報表上消失，風險卻仍然存在。

真正值得追求的，是問題能被看見、資料能被驗證、錯誤能被修正。

企業落實資料治理時，最難跨越的是技術、流程，還是管理文化？

One Misstep, Lasting Consequences: Why Data Governance Is Essential to Enterprise Risk Management

Before investing in ERP systems, big data analytics, or artificial intelligence, organizations should ask a more fundamental question: Can we trust the data in our systems?

The principle of “garbage in, garbage out” remains relevant. When source data is unreliable, even sophisticated analytics can turn flawed information into seemingly credible decisions.

Identity, Access, and Accountability

Consider several basic questions about identity and access management:

  • Are access rights promptly revoked when employees leave?
  • Are permissions adjusted when employees transfer roles or take a leave of absence?
  • Are accounts, access cards, and other credentials used only by their authorized holders?
  • Can system activity logs be traced to the individuals who actually performed each action?

When credentials are shared or transferred, the system’s record of who did what may no longer reflect reality. This weakens confidence in audit trails, which may then require additional verification before being used for accountability, risk assessment, or management decisions.

Governance Requires More Than Technology

Technical controls matter, but management commitment is equally important. When anomalies are discovered, is the organization willing to investigate causes, correct processes, and follow up? Or is the priority simply to submit reports on time and maintain attractive KPIs?

Practical steps include:

  1. Establishing access-management processes for onboarding, transfers, leaves, and departures.
  2. Preventing shared accounts and transferred credentials while maintaining traceable activity records.
  3. Regularly reviewing permissions and tracking anomalies through resolution.
  4. Creating an environment where employees can raise concerns safely and participate in improvements.

Trustworthy Data Matters Even More in the AI Era

As AI becomes part of forecasting, analysis, and decision-making, data quality and traceability must be treated as foundations rather than afterthoughts. This is a shared responsibility across IT, human resources, finance, audit, and leadership.

If an organization excludes the people who raise concerns, the issues may disappear from its reports while the underlying risks remain.

The real goal is to make problems visible, data verifiable, and mistakes correctable.

In implementing data governance, what is the greatest challenge: technology, processes, or management culture?
