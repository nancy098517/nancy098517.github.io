What Can You Take With You When You Leave a Job?

Nancy Lee · 2026 年 11 月 · 中文／English

一份工作可能持續十年、二十年，但人生不會隨著離職而結束。當我們離開熟悉的組織，過去累積的經驗，究竟有多少能夠成為下一段職涯的資產？這是我在多年工作經歷後，重新思考的一個問題。

中文版｜如果有一天離開現在的工作，你能帶走什麼？

今天看到一個廣告：「歡迎 30 歲以下、想換工作的人參加 XX 培訓。」

看完突然想到，如果現在要給年輕人一些職涯上的建議，我會說什麼？

認真想了一下，大概是這幾件事：

  1. 讓自己的職涯保有移動的可能。

    如果有機會，選擇能隨著人生需求調整地點、職務或發展方向的工作。不一定真的要離開，但最好讓自己一直保有選擇。

  2. 累積可以帶到下一個地方的專業。

    盡量讓自己的經驗能在同業、相近職務，甚至不同領域之間轉換。換工作不一定要歸零，過去累積的經驗，應該成為下一站的資產。

  3. 不管公司怎麼規劃你的未來，都不要忘記規劃自己的未來。

    公司有公司的考量，人生是自己的。即使工作很穩定，也不要停止學習，更不要把自己的成長完全交給一個組織決定。

  4. 偶爾問問自己：如果明天離開這份工作，我能帶走什麼？

    職稱未必帶得走，公司名稱也不屬於自己。真正能跟著我們走到下一站的，是能力、經驗、判斷力，以及重新開始的本事。

年輕的時候，我們常常想的是：

「怎麼找到一份好工作？」

現在的我反而覺得，也許還要多問一句：

「這份工作做了十年、二十年以後，會留下什麼在我身上？」

工作可以很久，人生更久。

不要只替公司累積經驗，也要替未來的自己累積選擇。

English｜What Can You Take With You When You Leave a Job?

Today, I came across an advertisement inviting people under 30 who are considering a career change to join a professional training program.

If I could offer a few pieces of career advice to younger professionals, what would I say?

After giving it some thought, these are the four things I would share:

  1. Build a career that gives you room to move.

    If possible, choose work that allows you to adapt as your life changes—whether that means changing locations, roles, or career directions. You may never need to make those changes, but it is valuable to know that you have options.

  2. Develop skills and experience that are transferable.

    Try to build expertise that can move with you—to another company, a similar role, or even a different industry. Changing jobs should not always mean starting from zero. Ideally, what you have learned should become an asset for your next chapter.

  3. No matter how a company plans for its future, remember to plan for yours.

    Organizations have their own priorities and considerations. Your career, however, is ultimately your responsibility. Even when a job feels stable, keep learning, growing, and developing capabilities that belong to you—not just to your current position.

  4. Ask yourself from time to time: If I left this job tomorrow, what could I take with me?

    Your title may not follow you. The company name does not belong to you. What stays with you are your skills, experience, judgment, and ability to start again.

When we are young, we often ask:

“How can I find a good job?”

Today, I think there is another question worth asking:

“After ten or twenty years in this job, what will remain with me?”

A job may last for many years.

Your career and your life last longer.

Don’t just accumulate experience for the company you work for.

Build choices for your future self.
